I raced on the IDF World Tour from 2014: 2nd overall in the 2019 European rankings, IDF European Racer Representative in 2020, BDSL champion, and 19th in the WDSC world rankings in 2022. In the scene I was the event guy, the one people asked what was on and where.

The sites that used to answer that question had gone dark. Freerides.org had been offline for years, the International Downhill Federation's website had not been updated since 2020, and social media only showed you events from organisers you already followed. So I built a calendar in WordPress, mainly for myself, and sent people the link instead of retyping the list. Far more riders used it to plan their summers than I expected.

That was the push to go back and study software. The calendar became my first Flask app, and then my final diploma project, rebuilt from scratch in Django and deployed on Heroku.

The Flask version is public on GitHub. The Django version's repository is private.
