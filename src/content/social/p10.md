GDPR erasure gets interesting when the data is meant to be public.

Our certificates have public verification pages. Anyone can check that a certificate is real.

So when someone asks to be erased, deleting the row isn't right, and keeping it isn't either.

What we do: scrub the name and personal data, keep the credential. The page still says "valid certificate". It no longer says whose.

Privacy rules belong in the model, not in a policy PDF.
