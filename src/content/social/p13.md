The first instinct with a pricing model is to lock it once a quote goes out.

Locks are a negotiation. Who can unlock it? What happens to the quote when someone does? Someone always needs to "just fix one thing".

In the pricing tool I built into our Django platform, nothing is locked. Every save of a scenario is a new, immutable revision. A quote pins the revision it was priced from and freezes its prices. The plan the business steers by pins one revision too, and adopting a new one is a recorded act by a person.

So people can edit freely, all day, and nothing that depends on a revision can see it change.

It's the same pattern I'd use for any document something else depends on: a published programme, a contract template, a certificate.

Don't protect the record from edits. Make edits create a new record.
