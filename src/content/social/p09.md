Before: course feedback at medical congresses meant paper forms, then someone typing them in, then someone building a report.

After:

Each session gets a QR code. Participants scan it and answer on their phone. No login.

Results show up on a live dashboard while the event is still running.

Sessions get benchmark percentiles against each other.

At the end, session and event-level PDF reports are generated automatically, with the event's branding. Charts are rendered server-side with matplotlib straight into the PDF via ReportLab. Export to CSV and Excel for anyone who wants the raw numbers.

Small Django app, 26 commits, now being folded into the main operations platform.

The bit people don't expect: server-side charts. No headless browser, no screenshot step. The PDF is the same every time.
