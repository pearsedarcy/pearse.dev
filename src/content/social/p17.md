Before I wrote software, I raced downhill skateboards.

From 2014 I raced the IDF World Tour, from Austria and the Czech Republic to France and the US. I won the British Downhill Skateboard League, finished second in the European rankings in 2019, and in 2020 the European racers elected me as their representative.

Racing taught me [VERIFY: the one habit from racing you still use at work, e.g. walking the course before you ride it, or changing one thing at a time].

It also made me the event guy. Riders asked me what was on that summer, so I built an events calendar in WordPress. Then I rebuilt it in Flask to learn, and in Django for my diploma. That calendar is the reason I write software for a living.

Today I'm the engineer on MTS Hub and wetlabs.app at MTS – The Wetlab Company in Innsbruck. Different hills, same habits.
