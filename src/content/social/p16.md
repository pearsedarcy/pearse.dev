I built the same app three times, and it's the reason I write software for a living.

In downhill skateboarding I was the event guy. Riders asked me what was on that summer, and I answered with a rough calendar typed into DMs. Again and again.

The sites that used to answer that question had gone dark. So, injured and out of work, I built Skatedownhills: an events calendar in WordPress, mostly for myself.

Its first summer, riders I had never met thanked me for it. That stuck.

When I went back to study, it became my first Flask app. Then my final diploma project, rebuilt from scratch in Django: events, profiles, calendar views with filters and maps, email notifications.

WordPress taught me to ship. Flask showed me what a framework does by making me do it myself. Django let me spend my time on the calendar instead of the plumbing.

Build the thing you'd use yourself first. Then build it again.
