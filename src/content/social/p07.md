One rule shows up in every platform I build: nothing generated reaches a real person without a human approving it.

In the operations platform, it's a decision record: no email sends without human approval. In the content tool, AI drafts and a person approves by email before anything ships.

It costs a click. It buys you every mistake caught before it's someone else's problem.

Self-hosting the model keeps the data in. The approval step keeps the errors in.
