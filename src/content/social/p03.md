Before: packing for a travelling wetlab event meant paper lists and tribal knowledge. What goes in which crate for which course depended on who had packed it last time.

After:

Courses are planned as rooms → stations → equipment.

Packing lists are generated from templates per session. Deterministic: same course, same list.

A live packing screen over WebSockets lets several people work the same checklist at once and see each other's ticks.

Serialised equipment is tracked. Vehicle capacity is known before the van is loaded.

This started as a small tool, WetPack, in February. It's now part of the operations platform I'm building in Django, alongside bookings, staffing and certificates.

The lesson: the paper list wasn't the problem. The problem was that the knowledge of what goes on it lived in people. Templates moved it into the system.
