November 2023: I wrote my first HTML and CSS page.

February 2024: a JavaScript app that splits the cost of a trip between friends.

March 2024: a Python CLI for knockout tournament brackets.

December 2026: I'm the engineer on a Django operations platform with 6000+ tests, a surgical video platform live at congresses, and a team of AI agents I run with written rules and enforced hooks.

What got me from one to the other wasn't talent. It was building in public, small and often, and writing down why I made each decision. Most of those early projects are still public on my GitHub. They're rough. I leave them up on purpose.

If you're early in this and wondering whether the walkthrough projects matter: they do. Not because anyone reads them, but because you did them.
