My first real Django app wasn't for work. It was for downhill skateboarding.

[VERIFY: the itch, e.g. events scattered across group chats.] So in December 2024 I started an events calendar: profiles, comments and reviews, filtering, email reminders. About 120 commits later it's still going.

Everything I now do at work started there. Models that match how people actually talk about the thing. An admin someone else can use. Email that goes out when it should, and only then.

Moving from Dublin to Innsbruck [VERIFY: when you moved] was partly about the work and partly about the hills. There are a lot more of them here.

The lesson I keep relearning: build the thing you'd use yourself first. You find out fast what's annoying.
