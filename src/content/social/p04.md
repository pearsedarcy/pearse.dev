Genuine question for people running AI in production.

For segmenting surgical training videos into procedure phases, I had two options:

A. A general vision-language model (Qwen3-VL, self-hosted via Ollama). Ask it what phase it's looking at.

B. A small supervised model (a frozen image encoder under an MS-TCN++ temporal model), trained locally, judged against a locked test set.

I went with B as the default and kept A as opt-in. A gives plausible answers. B gives a number I can defend, and I know when it gets worse.

But B costs labelled data and training time, and A gets better every few months without me doing anything.

Where do you draw that line? At what point would you switch back to the general model?
